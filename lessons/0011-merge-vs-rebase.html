Lesson 0011 · Phase 3: Staying in sync · 14 min

Updating your branch: merge or rebase?

You will be able to: choose between merge and rebase for a given branch and know the golden rule.

Staging moved on; your PR says out of date. You must bring staging's new commits into your branch.

The golden rule

Never rebase commits that other people have based work on. Rebase changes commit ids; teammates who already have the old ones will be forced to reconcile.

If unsure: merge. It is always safe. Rebase is a tidiness choice, not a necessity.
