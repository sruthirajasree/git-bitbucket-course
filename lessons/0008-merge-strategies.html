Merge strategies: merge commit, squash, fast-forward

You will be able to: predict what staging's history looks like after each strategy and how your local branch will look.

The merge dialog may offer several strategies. Each produces a different shape on staging.

Merge commit

Squash

Fast-forward

StrategyHistoryBest forWatch out
Merge commitComplete, branchyMulti-commit work you want to keepNoisy graph
SquashOne commit per PRMessy WIP commits; simple revert (one commit)Local branch looks unmerged; individual commits lost
Fast-forward / rebaseLinearClean historyRewrites commit ids; needs up-to-date branch
Bitbucket: Admins may restrict the allowed strategies (Data Center offers merge commit, fast-forward, FF-only, rebase variants, squash variants). Check the dropdown; the repo default is your team's convention.
Reverting is easiest after a squash (one commit, git revert <sha>) and trickiest after a merge commit (git revert -m 1 <merge-sha>, lesson 19).
